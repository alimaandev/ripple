# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that build an open, welcoming,
friendly, safe, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being beginner-friendly. New contributors are the future of this project —
  answer questions patiently and point at the right files.
- Respecting others' perspectives and viewpoints.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Using sexualized language or imagery, or making unwelcome sexual advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing results of others' private information without explicit permission.
- Behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported by contacting the project
maintainer at [alishermaan0319@gmail.com](mailto:alishermaan0319@gmail.com).
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated in a way that respects the
privacy and safety of the reporter. Enforcement responsibilities are
clarified to the role of maintainers who decide what action is appropriate
in response.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may take any of the following steps for a violation:

1. A private, written warning with a clear explanation.
2. A public apology or permission correction.
3. A temporary or permanent ban from interacting with the project.
